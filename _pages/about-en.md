I am Huifang Ma, a Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor at the <a href="https://jsj.nwnu.edu.cn/" class="institution-link"><img src="{{ '/images/nwnu-emblem.png' | relative_url }}" class="institution-icon" alt=""><span>College of Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science, Northwest Normal University</span></a>. My core research focuses on intelligent computing for graph data, with applications spanning [Bioinformatics](#bioinformatics), [AI in Education](#cognitive-diagnosis), [Intelligent Industrial Diagnosis](#industrial-diagnosis), and [other areas](#graph-modeling). As a corresponding author, I have published multiple papers in top-tier journals and conferences (e.g., [TKDD](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tkdd), [TGRS](https://www.grss-ieee.org/publications/transactions-on-geoscience-remote-sensing/), [AAAI](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/)), with over [4,600 Google Scholar citations](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=r4iH7uIAAAAJ&hl=en). I currently serve as a Senior Editor for *[Expert Systems with Applications](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/expert-systems-with-applications)*.
