I am currently a Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor at the <a href="https://jsj.nwnu.edu.cn/" class="institution-link"><img src="{{ '/images/nwnu-emblem.png' | relative_url }}" class="institution-icon" alt="">College of Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science, Northwest Normal University</a>. I received my Ph.D. in Engineering from the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2010 and joined Northwest Normal University in the same year. I was promoted to Professor in 2018.

My research interests focus on **intelligent computing for graph data** and its applications in **bioinformatics modeling, intelligent education, intelligent industrial diagnosis, and software vulnerability mining**. In recent years, I have published numerous papers as the corresponding author in internationally recognized journals and conferences, including [TKDD](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tkdd), [TGRS](https://www.grss-ieee.org/publications/transactions-on-geoscience-remote-sensing/), [TII](https://www.ieee-ies.org/pubs/transactions-on-industrial-informatics), [AAAI](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/), [ICDM](https://icdm2026.neu.edu.cn/main.htm), [CIKM](https://www.cikmconference.org/), and [SDM](https://www.siam.org/conferences-events/siam-conferences/sdm26/). These include 20 papers in SCI Q1 Top journals. My work has received more than [4,200 Google Scholar citations](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=r4iH7uIAAAAJ&hl=en).

In academic service, I have served as a Senior Program Committee Member for international conferences including [ACM MM](https://www.acmmm.org/) 2023–2025, [ACL](https://2023.aclweb.org/) 2021–2023, and [EMNLP](https://2026.emnlp.org/) 2021–2023. I currently serve as a Senior Editor of *[Expert Systems with Applications](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/expert-systems-with-applications)*. I also serve as an Executive Committee Member of the Technical Committee on Artificial Intelligence and Pattern Recognition of the China Computer Federation, a Member of the Technical Committee on Mind Computation of the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence, and a Youth Member of its Technical Committee on Knowledge Engineering and Distributed Intelligence.
